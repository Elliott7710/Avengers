FlowGuard is intended for small businesses worldwide, with Malaysia as the first operating country. The user's planned minimum age is 16+, subject to checking local requirements in each market. The app is planned to be free for now, with no payments or refund policy in the initial scope. No legal entity is registered yet. No public support contact has been selected; privacy requests should be submitted through an in-app form and processed by FlowGuard operators via Supabase admin access.

**Why:** These are product and business constraints supplied by the user; legal drafts and launch decisions must not invent them or treat them as final jurisdictional advice.

**How to apply:** Use these facts when planning signup, policies, monetization, and launch readiness. Keep privacy requests separate from customer-manager access, apply/test the staging migration before launch, and review laws in Malaysia and every additional target market before finalizing legal text.
