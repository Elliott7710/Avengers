Travel

Requests, destinations, and approvals

Open Travel
How do I create a travel request?

Open Travel, select New request, fill in the required fields, then choose Add request. Your request will appear in the list below the summary boxes.

Can I enter a city or a custom destination?

Yes. Start typing to see matching country suggestions, or enter any city, region, or other destination. Suggestions do not restrict what you can save.

How do I update a request or its status?

Select a request row to edit it. Change its details or status, then save. To remove a request, open it and choose Delete.

Insights

Team trends and coaching context

Open Insights
What should an Insights entry contain?

Choose a team, add the person or team name, period, metric, and value. A coaching note is optional and can provide context for a conversation.

Can I edit or remove an Insights entry?

Select its row to open the entry. From there you can save changes or delete it.

Are Insights individual performance scores?

No. The module is intended for team-level work trends and coaching prompts, not surveillance scores.

Workspace and data

What this preview currently supports

Where is my data saved?

Travel requests and Insights entries are saved in this browser on this device. They are not synced to an account or shared with other users. Clearing browser site data may remove them.

Does Create account or Sign in create an account?

Not yet. Those screens are visual previews; there is no account service or server connection in this version.
