# FlowGuard workflow demo insert

## Director's treatment

- **Promise:** A travel request becomes a traceable, human-reviewed outcome.
- **Identity:** FlowGuard's deep blue-green and warm white glass UI, with a restrained mint signal and a small amber budget marker. Use Space Grotesk for large editorial type and DM Sans for interface copy; monospace labels provide system-like precision.
- **Hero:** A synthetic travel request card that changes state as it passes from employee to independent manager and finally into a visible activity record.
- **Motion:** Camera push-throughs on the request ID, a clipped split-screen reveal between employee and manager, and a drawn activity path that resolves into the FlowGuard mark.
- **Arc:** A sharp coordination-problem hook accelerates into request validation, pauses on the illustrative budget flag, then gives the manager's decision and logged outcome more air.
- **Payoff:** The UI collapses into a tiny four-step recording cue: submit, flag, manager, history. It is a practical instruction for the person recording, not a product CTA.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.
- **Runtime:** 24.0 seconds.

## Variety and accuracy review

The adjacent shots change framing and visual mechanic: layered panes/push-through; close paper-form field construction; long threshold diagram; split human review; centred paper activity record and inverse end-card. The story remains joined by `TRV-014`, a traced connector, and the FlowGuard petrol/mint/amber vocabulary. Every shot develops after its first reveal, and each outgoing move begins before its final 15–20% so no empty tail is left. The illustrative MYR 1,000 threshold is explicitly qualified as demo-only; no claim of measured savings, production policy, live access isolation, successful AI inference, or real booking is made.

## Creator recording cue

When filming yourself, use synthetic data only. Record the employee flow through submission and the `Requested`/illustrative flag, switch to the separate manager session for a reasoned decision, then return to show the requester outcome and activity history. Keep the AI summary and actual booking out of this insert; those capabilities are not established as successful/live in the supplied runbook and evidence.
